How it works

From raw signal to clinical action, automatically

Predix Health sits quietly in the background of existing care — no new hardware, no workflow overhaul.

Data comes in continuously

Vitals from wearables sync alongside lab results and history already in the EHR — one unified feed per patient.

The model looks for patterns

It compares longitudinal signals against each patient’s baseline to surface meaningful changes for clinical review.

The doctor gets an early alert

A plain-language summary with the flagged risk and a suggested next step — reviewed, never auto-actioned.

Sample clinician dashboard

What the clinician actually sees

A single panel per patient — vitals, trend and risk level, with one clear next action when something needs attention.

Why it holds up in practice
